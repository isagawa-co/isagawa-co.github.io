The Problem

50 applications. 50 unique forms. One exhausted human.

Every job has a different application form. Copy your resume into 15 text boxes. Re-type your address. Upload the same PDF again. Miss one required field and start over. You spend more time filling forms than finding jobs.

  • How do you apply to 50 jobs without burning out on repetitive forms?
  • How do you avoid typos and missed fields that cost you interviews?
  • How do you keep every application consistent and complete?

How It Works

Four steps. From job posting to submitted application.

Point the agent at a job application. It reads every field, matches your profile data, fills the form, and waits for your review. You approve, it submits. Every field accounted for.

1

Discover Form

The agent navigates to the job application page. It reads every field — text inputs, dropdowns, file uploads, checkboxes. It maps the entire form structure before touching anything.

2

Match Profile

Your profile contains your resume, work history, education, skills, and common answers. The agent matches each form field to the right piece of your profile. No guessing, no hallucinating.

3

Fill & Review

The agent fills every field. You see exactly what it wrote before anything is submitted. Change a word, swap an answer, add a custom note. You stay in control of every application.

4

Submit

One click to submit. The agent handles file uploads, confirmation checkboxes, and multi-page forms. Every submission is logged so you can track what you sent and where.
