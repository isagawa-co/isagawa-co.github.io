How It Works

Four phases. From idea to working project.

Answer simple questions about your app. Pick the technology you want. Get a complete project with built-in rules. The agent enforces its own architecture as it builds your features.

1

Discovery

Answer 4 plain English questions about your app. What does it do? Who uses it? How do they interact with it? What's the main problem it solves? The AI learns everything it needs to build your project.

2

Decisions

The AI presents technology options for your project. Frontend, backend, database, deployment. Pick what you understand or like. The AI explains each choice in everyday language, not jargon.

3

Scaffold

The AI creates a complete working project. Folder structure. Configuration. Base code. Everything ready to run. The architecture is locked in. The AI enforces it on every new feature you request.

4

Features

Describe new features in plain English. "Add a login page." "Send email notifications." The AI builds them. Every feature follows the same architecture. Your codebase stays clean and maintainable.
